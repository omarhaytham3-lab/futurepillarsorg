What We Do

Our Pillar: Guiding the Lights

Future Pillars Organization has run many programmes since 2022 — school mentorship, GBV awareness, civic engagement, digital literacy, career guidance. Today, our work is centered on one flagship: the Guiding the Lights Leadership Academy (GTLLA) — now a fully digital programme, having started as a hybrid one.

Our Current Focus

Guiding the Lights Leadership Academy

GTLLA is our only ongoing programme. Cohort 1 (hybrid: in-person and online) graduated 10 leaders in January 2025. Cohort 2 — now fully digital — has 20 mentees enrolled and is set to graduate in December 2026.

About Us

Our Story

Future Pillars Organization was founded in Mombasa, Kenya, officially registered on 19 September 2022. It grew out of a recognition that too many young people and women in coastal communities were disconnected from the civic and governance structures meant to serve them. What began as a small mentorship effort has grown into a community-based organization — marking its first anniversary in September 2023 with the opening of a permanent office in Old Town, Mombasa.

Founder & Executive Director

HI

Haytam Isse

Executive Director

Haytam made Mombasa his home and, in September 2022, brought together a small group of volunteers for a first meeting that became Future Pillars Organization — driven by a conviction that Mombasa's young people needed more than good intentions; they needed mentors who would keep showing up. That first mentorship session at Allidina Visram High School has since grown into the Guiding the Lights Leadership Academy and national recognition: Haytam was named among Kenya's 100 Most Influential Muslims by The Muslim Media in 2023, and profiled by DW Swahili in 2024 for his work in youth education. He continues to lead Future Pillars' flagship programme today.

Mission & Vision

Mission: To guide young people in Mombasa toward leadership through sustained, hands-on mentorship — pairing them with mentors who walk alongside them from first conversation to graduation and beyond.

Vision: A Mombasa where every young person has walked through a door like Guiding the Lights — mentored, equipped, and ready to lead in their school, their ward, and their community.

Core Values

Mentorship First

Every programme we run comes back to one thing: a mentor showing up, consistently, for a young person.

Community Ownership

Programmes are built with schools, youth councils, and local leaders — not delivered to them from outside.

Consistency

We track our work session by session — because showing up once doesn't change a life; showing up every week does.

Partnership

From county government to grassroots youth groups, our impact multiplies through who we work alongside.

Integrity

We do what we say, and account for what we do.

Growth

From one mentorship session in 2022 to a full leadership academy today — we keep building on what works.

Our Journey

19 September 2022

Future Pillars Organization founded

Officially registered in Mombasa, Kenya, following an initial organizing meeting later that month.

October 2022

First GBV response & awareness training

Ran a sexual and gender-based violence awareness and training programme for members and volunteers; responded to the organization's first GBV case within weeks of founding.

March 2023

Mkomani Youth Council launched; Uzalendo Afrika partnership begins

Launched the Mkomani Youth Council (20 council members, 200 community members in attendance) and began a sustained partnership with Uzalendo Afrika Initiative — the first of many joint events through the year.

March 2023

Mentorship programme begins at Allidina Visram

First mentorship session held with 40 students, expanding to the whole school by May 2023 after the success of the Form 1C pilot class.
